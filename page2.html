What is inside?

Pistachios are packed with nutrients. They're a great source of protein, healthy fats, fiber, and various vitamins and minerals.

Read More

More Information

  • Protein: A handful of pistachios can provide a good amount of protein, which is essential for muscle repair and growth.
  • Healthy Fats: They contain heart-healthy monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats.
  • Fiber: Great for digestion and maintaining healthy gut flora.
  • Antioxidants: Pistachios are rich in antioxidants, which help protect your cells from damage.
